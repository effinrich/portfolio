# Typography

Two families, both relying on a system fallback chain (no webfont ships — the OS provides Inter /
JetBrains Mono or substitutes). Body opts into Inter's stylistic alternates
(`font-feature-settings: "ss01", "cv11"`) and `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased`.
